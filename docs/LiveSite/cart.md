# How to see your cart product?

- You can see your cart menu on the top right corner of the page. click on the cart menu to redireact to cart page.

![cart](./img/c2.png)

- You can checkout by clicking on the Proceed to checkout button. 
- After clicking on the button you can see the checkout page.
- Here you can fill up the form and click on the Place order button to place the order.
- You can apply coupon to get discount.
