# Notices

The Notice section allows coaches to send important updates and information to service.
Coaches can create and send notices to services, ensuring that all members receive the necessary information.

![Notice](./img/ser_no.png)
<!-- image -->
# Here is how to send a notice!

To send a notice, click on the **Add Notice** button. A form will appear where coaches can write the notice title and description. To click on the **Submit** button to Submit the notice.

<!-- image -->
# Here is how to edit and delete a notice!

To edit and delete a notice, click on the  **Edit Action** buttons. To click on the **Delete Action** button to delete the notice.
